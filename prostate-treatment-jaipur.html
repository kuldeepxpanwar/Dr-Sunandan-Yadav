Prostate Treatment in Jaipur

An enlarged prostate (BPH) is a common, non-cancerous condition in men above 50 that slowly disturbs urination and sleep. Dr. Sunandan Yadav, Consultant Urologist in Jaipur, evaluates prostate problems at Pratap Nagar and treats them with medicines first, and surgery only when truly needed.

Symptoms of an enlarged prostate

  • Waking up repeatedly at night to pass urine
  • Weak or interrupted urine stream, difficulty starting
  • Frequent urge, or feeling the bladder never fully empties
  • In later stages: urine retention, infections or bladder stones

How it is evaluated

Evaluation usually includes a clinical examination, sonography of the prostate and bladder, urine flow assessment and blood tests such as PSA when indicated. This separates simple enlargement from infection or other causes.

Treatment options

  • Medicines — relax the prostate/bladder neck or shrink the prostate over time; effective for many men.
  • Surgery — advised when medicines fail or complications arise; modern options include endoscopic and laser procedures with faster recovery.
  • Watchful follow-up — for very mild symptoms, periodic review may be enough.

Prostate symptoms overlap with other urology problems, including stones and infections — a proper evaluation avoids wrong self-treatment.

Common questions

At what age do prostate problems usually start?

Prostate enlargement (BPH) is common after the age of 50 and becomes more frequent with age. Urinary symptoms at any age should still be evaluated, as infections and other causes are also possible.

Can enlarged prostate be treated with medicines only?

Many men improve with medicines alone, especially in early stages. Surgery is considered when medicines stop working, urine blocks completely, or complications like repeated infections or bladder stones appear.

What are the surgical options for prostate enlargement?

Depending on prostate size and your health, procedures such as TURP or laser prostate surgery may be advised. The doctor explains the suitable option after examination, sonography and flow tests.

Is prostate enlargement the same as prostate cancer?

No. BPH is a non-cancerous enlargement. However, both can cause similar urinary symptoms, so evaluation — sometimes including a PSA blood test — is important to rule out other problems.

When should I see a doctor urgently for prostate symptoms?

If you are completely unable to pass urine, have fever with urinary symptoms, or see blood in urine, seek care immediately.
