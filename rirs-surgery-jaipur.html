RIRS Surgery in Jaipur — Scarless Laser Stone Removal

RIRS (Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery) is the modern, scarless way to remove kidney stones: a thin flexible scope reaches the kidney through the natural urinary passage, and a laser breaks the stone into fine pieces. Dr. Sunandan Yadav performs advanced minimally invasive stone surgery, including RIRS, at Jaipur.

Who is RIRS suitable for?

  • Kidney stones up to about 2 cm
  • Stones that did not pass with medicines
  • Patients who want to avoid cuts and visible scars
  • Stones in positions difficult to reach with other methods

How the procedure works

Under anaesthesia, a flexible scope is passed through the urinary passage into the kidney — no cut is made on the body. The stone is located, broken into small fragments with a laser, and the pieces are removed or pass naturally. A temporary stent may be placed and removed later in a short visit.

Recovery

Most patients are discharged the next day and return to light routine within a few days. Drinking adequate water and completing follow-up reviews help prevent new stones. For larger stones, other options like PCNL may be more suitable — this is decided from your scan.

Common questions

Is RIRS surgery painful?

RIRS is done under anaesthesia, so you do not feel pain during the procedure. Mild burning while passing urine or slight discomfort for a few days afterwards is common and settles with medicines.

How long does RIRS take, and when can I go home?

The procedure usually takes under an hour for most stones. Many patients are discharged the next day, depending on their condition and the doctor's assessment.

Will there be a stent after RIRS?

A thin temporary stent is often placed to help the kidney drain while it heals. It is removed in a short follow-up visit, usually after 1–2 weeks.
